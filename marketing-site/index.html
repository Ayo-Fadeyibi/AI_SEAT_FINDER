01

The problem isn't a lack of seats.

It's not knowing which ones are actually free.
Pain point 01
20–30 min

Searching, floor by floor

Students walk the building blind, checking desk after desk — a routine that gets worse exactly when the library is busiest, during midterms and finals.

Pain point 02
Hours, unused

Hoarding nobody enforces

A bag on the chair and a book on the desk hold a seat for hours while the student who claimed it is somewhere else entirely.

Pain point 03
Zero visibility

No way to know until you look

There's no signal for whether an empty-looking seat is truly free, briefly stepped away from, or sitting next to the outlet and quiet zone you actually need.

Who's building this.

A small team that got tired of walking three floors for a chair.

We're building toward becoming the resource-management layer smart campuses reach for first — starting with the library, because that's where the problem was most obvious.
